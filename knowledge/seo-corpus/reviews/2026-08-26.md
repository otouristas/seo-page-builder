[Only 25% of cited sources overlap between ChatGPT‘s different reasoning modes Study](https://www.semrush.com/blog/chatgpt-reasoning-ai-visibility/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) | Major measurement update: **“ChatGPT visibility” is not one metric.** Benchmark by model/mode + prompt + intent stage. Crawlability, content and indexability come first. |

### Most important change to the library

The emerging evidence now supports a stricter **GEO evidence hierarchy**:

**Google-confirmed eligibility/requirements → controlled experiments → longitudinal observational studies → cross-sectional correlations → practitioner anecdotes.**

That distinction matters. Semrush's 5M-URL study, for example, finds AI-cited pages over-index for schema, moderate-length URLs and stronger engagement—but the authors themselves acknowledge correlation rather than causation and note that engagement occurs *after* citation. Those patterns are useful hypotheses for experiments, **not new ranking factors or rules such as “make every slug 17–40 characters.”** [How Do Technical SEO Factors Impact AI Search? Study](https://www.semrush.com/blog/technical-seo-impact-on-ai-search-study/)

I’ve carried these forward as **net-new corpus additions**, with the Google/vendor/evidence labels preserved so later recommendations don't accidentally turn correlations into “Google says” claims.
