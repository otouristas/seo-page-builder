## File format

Each file opens with a YAML front-matter block carrying the SEO fields (meta title, meta description, slug, primary and secondary keywords, schema recommendation, internal links to include, image brief with ALT text). The article body follows in Markdown: H1, H2 sections roughly every 200–250 words, a visible FAQ block with H3 questions, and a closing CTA section with the internal links.

Paste the body into the CMS and use the front matter for the SEO and schema fields. FAQ blocks are written to be rendered on the page so FAQPage markup can be applied; drop the markup if the FAQs are hidden.

## Editorial decisions

- **Length follows the brief tabs (1,600–2,300 words), not the general brand guide's 700–1,400 editorial range.** The briefs specify nine or ten sections plus FAQs for each article and call them specification guides. Cutting to 1,100 words would drop sections the briefs ask for.
- **Spelling follows glm-outdoor.com.** "Louvers" matches the product pages and the target keywords; everything else is British English (aluminium, colour, metres, specialise).
- **Voice.** Scene-led openings, benefit framing and sensory language from the brand guide, on a specification-led structure from the briefs. No feature dumps, no hype vocabulary, no generic CTAs. Every product claim below is sourced from a GLM page.
- **"GLM Outdoor Solutions"** appears once per article, then "GLM".
